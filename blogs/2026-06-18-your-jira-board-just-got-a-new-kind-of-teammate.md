AI agents are now integrated as first-class teammates in Jira, where they can be assigned work items, mentioned in comments, connected from third-party platforms, and scaled through automation rules. The post walks through implementation examples such as overnight bug fixes, quarterly planning assistance, and marketing campaign workflows, plus step-by-step guidance for building agents in Rovo Studio, wiring them into workflows, setting up automations, testing, and monitoring performance.
