Bitbucket Packages now supports immutable tags, enabling workspace admins to lock container image tags after initial push for improved reproducibility, deployment safety, and compliance audit trails. This feature helps teams enforce consistent image versioning and prevents accidental or unauthorized overwrites of published container images.
