The post introduces the Knowledge Architect, a new organizational role that bridges documented organizational history with AI-augmented operations through context engineering, structuring and delivering information so both humans and AI agents make high-quality decisions. Key responsibilities include designing context flow across teams, defining human-AI engagement rules, upskilling knowledge workers, and building infrastructure like taxonomies and governance frameworks, scaling from a single person in smaller organizations to distributed teams in enterprises.
