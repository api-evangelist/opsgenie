Ace Hardware, the 102-year-old co-op, migrated from legacy email-based ticketing to Jira Service Management with deep integrations across Salesforce, Azure, and other systems, enabling standardized workflows across 5,200 stores. The transformation demonstrates how even large traditional enterprises can modernize IT service delivery using integrated tooling across a distributed retail network.
