Wendy's scaled Atlassian from 147 to nearly 1,000 users in under two years through organic adoption rather than top-down mandates, leveraging change management and practical solutions across business and IT teams. The case study illustrates how everyday operational challenges, when solved with collaborative tooling, can become the foundation for enterprise-wide digital transformation.
